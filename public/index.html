🧪 Lab limitations

The browser simulator executes a curated subset of DAX against a small sample dataset. It is designed to teach syntax and concepts; it is not a complete VertiPaq / Analysis Services engine. Complex context transitions, relationships, and model metadata need a real DAX engine to validate exactly.
